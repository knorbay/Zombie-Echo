# Zombie Echo: Those Left in the Dark

## Short description

A dark, fast top-down arena shooter where close-range kills keep your momentum alive.

## Page description

The arena is dark, the waves are short, and standing still is the fastest way to die.

**Zombie Echo: Those Left in the Dark** is a compact top-down shooter built around movement and pressure. Dash through enemies, finish weakened targets with a melee strike, chain kills for extra speed, and turn every close-range elimination into ammunition and a faster dash recovery.

### What is in the game

- Seven distinct weapons, from a reliable pistol to an explosive launcher
- Fast enemy waves with runners, tanks, stalkers, spitters, screamers, and exploders
- A different boss archetype every five waves
- Dash damage, melee executions, explosive barrels, and combo rewards
- Weapon drops, ammo, medkits, temporary boosts, and wave rewards
- Sparse lighting and limited visibility that keep the action readable without making the arena safe

There are no rarity tiers, account systems, or progression grind. Pick up a weapon and survive as long as you can.

### Controls

- **WASD:** Move
- **Left mouse button:** Fire
- **R:** Reload
- **1-7 / mouse wheel:** Switch weapon
- **Space:** Dash
- **Q / right mouse button:** Melee
- **H:** Use medkit
- **I / Tab:** Open loadout
- **E:** Pick up a weapon
- **Esc:** Pause

### Download note

Downloads are available for Windows x64, Linux x64, and Apple Silicon Macs. Mac users should extract the ZIP, then right-click **Zombie Echo.app** and choose **Open** the first time if macOS displays an unidentified developer warning.
